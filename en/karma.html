The questions this report answers

This report focuses solely on the question: "What was I born to learn and what role was I meant to take on in this life?" rather than evaluating your personality or fortune. While the Lifetime Report covers all areas of life, this one specializes in deciphering your mission, utilizing elements not deeply covered in the comprehensive version, such as your Atmakaraka, Navamsha (D9), and the nodal axis.

What are your innate Karma, challenges, and trials? From Ketu, which shows "what you have already mastered and tend to revert to," and Rahu and Saturn, which show "areas that are immature but need to be developed," you will understand the types of challenges you tend to repeat in life and their meanings.
What is your mission? From the 9th house (beliefs and guidance), the 10th house (acts returned to the world), and your Amatyakaraka, we will decipher what roles give you strength.
What is it that only you can do? From your Karakamsha in the Navamsha (D9) and the trines of Dharma, we will tell you your unique combinations that no one else can replace.
In what moments do you feel truly alive? From the Moon, the deities of your Nakshatra, and the 5th house, you will discover the scenes that bring peace and joy to your soul, the conditions that drain your vitality, and the small daily actions that return you to joy.

Structure of the report (approx. 55 pages)

  1. Blueprint of your soul (Overview) The reason why this birth chart (Janma Kundali) is not a design that everyone has, your challenges and mission in a single word, and your life purpose described in a sentence that brings an everyday scene to mind.
  2. Who were you born to be? Lagna and Atmakaraka — the purpose of your soul, the fundamental desires you want to fulfill in this life, and the habits that appear when you are immature.
  3. Your innate Karma Ketu — what you are naturally good at and can do unconsciously, your tendency to revert to it, and what you no longer need to hold on to.
  4. Challenges and trials in this life Rahu and Saturn — areas to develop, challenges that take time to overcome, and the power you gain once you overcome these trials.
  5. When trials will come, and their meaning Sade Sati and Dasha — what was forged in the past, how your current period relates to your challenges, and what you will learn in future periods (on a yearly basis).
  6. Direction of your mission 9th house, 10th house, and Amatyakaraka — what you believe in, what you convey, and the role you play in the world.
  7. What only you can do Karakamsha and the trines of Dharma — your unique combinations and the signs that show you are on your true path.
  8. Moments you feel truly alive Moon, 5th house, and the deities of your Nakshatra — the patterns of your joy, conditions where you easily lose vitality, and small daily rituals.
  9. What you must accomplish in this life (Summary) Your calling combining the findings of this report, the side paths where you easily get lost, and the first step to take in your current Dasha.

At the end of the report, we include a list of the placements used (planets, houses, signs, Nakshatra, and dignity), your Atmakaraka and Karakamsha, your Rahu / Ketu axis, your Sade Sati periods, a Dasha timeline, and definitions of terms.
