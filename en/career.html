Questions this report answers

Many career assessments end with "You are a ◯◯ type." What this report answers is, "In which job will you not feel effort as a struggle, be evaluated by others, and accumulate income?" We define a true calling as a state where the qualities shown in your birth chart (Janma Kundali) align with the actual work, working style, and earning methods you engage in—self-alignment—and answer the following questions one by one.

What are your suitable professions? Not abstract "types," but multiple specific profession names. Each includes the reasons in your birth chart (Janma Kundali) and how to exert your strength in that job. Unsuitable professions are also clearly stated separately.
What brings out your strength? Conditions that bring out your strength, tasks that don't feel like a burden, moments of immersion, and conversely, causes of exhaustion are detailed separately.
How are you seen by others? How you appear to those around you, what is actually evaluated, strengths you are unaware of, and points where you are easily misunderstood.
Should you be employed, independent, or partner up? From the 6th, 7th, and 10th houses, which working style brings self-alignment. Also, conditions to meet before changing your working style.
How does money come in, and where does it leak? Earning patterns, easy income routes, what gets priced, growth cycles, saving ability, and spending habits.
When will things move? Your current flowing cycle (Dasha), and the months when your career and wealth will move or remain static in the next 12 months.
