1. The decisive difference from Western astrology: the sidereal method, aligned with real space

The greatest difference between Western astrology and Vedic astrology (Jyotish) lies in how the ecliptic — the path of the stars — is calculated.

■ Western astrology (tropical method)

Calculated from the vernal equinox of some 2,000 years ago, fixed to the cycle of the seasons.

■ Vedic astrology (sidereal method)

Corrects the drift caused by the tilt of the Earth's axis (precession) down to the smallest unit, and calculates from the real positions of the planets in tonight's sky.

Because of the Earth's precession, the real sky and the calendar signs have drifted apart by about 24 degrees (this precise correction value is called the Ayanamsa).

LIBERTAS JYOTISH computes the astronomical Ayanamsa with great care. From the real signs and planetary degrees actually present in the sky above you tonight, we build your own cosmic blueprint (your horoscope, the Rashi Chakra) exactly as it is — not as comforting words.

2. Three specialist systems of interpretation behind the precision

We present this as the most refined divination in the world because of the depth of its layered calculation and logic.

① The 27 lunar mansions, the Nakshatras

Going beyond the twelve signs of Western astrology (a coarse division of 30 degrees each), we use the Nakshatras, which divide the Moon's path into 27 finer regions. They bring the deep psychology hidden within a single sign, the habits of your subconscious, your patterns in relationships and the true purpose of your soul into microscopic focus.

② Vimshottari Dasha, the precise timeline of your life

Dasha is Vedic astrology's own timing system, and it identifies when the turning points of your life will arrive with remarkable precision. Starting from the Moon's Nakshatra at the moment of your birth, it calculates in layers which planet's influence you are living under, and works out the decisive moments and strategies of your life: when to push, and when to consolidate.

③ Lagna, which defines the vessel of your soul

Using not only your date of birth but a confirmed time of birth and the precise latitude and longitude of your birthplace, we calculate exactly which point the eastern horizon was facing at the moment you were born — your Lagna (ascendant, or first house). With the Lagna as the axis, the true structure of a person, woven by the nine planets (Navagraha) and the twelve houses, can be analysed in three dimensions.
