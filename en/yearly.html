The Questions This Report Answers

What people reading an annual fortune really want to know is not "Is this a good year?", but "Can I execute that decision I am thinking about this year? If so, in which month?" This report answers by naming the months for the following 6 actions, based on your birth chart (Janma Kundali), this year's planetary movements (transits), and your current cycle (Dasha).

Job Change / Independence Announcing resignation, taking a new position, or starting a business. If there is a month with a push to start under your own name, that will also be noted.
Moving Relocating or signing a housing contract.
Marriage / Cohabitation / Promises with a Partner Exchanging promises or starting to live together.
Parting / Letting Go Announcing the end of a relationship or letting go of belongings.
Relearning / Qualifications / Traveling Far Applying or departing.
Major Purchases / Borrowing / Asset Movements Signing contracts or making payments.

It writes not the month to "decide" in your heart, but the month to actually execute it outwardly. Even when reading a month not to execute, it does not end with "be careful" or "prepare well," but shows the astrological reasons why that month should be avoided.

How to Read

  • The month to execute is the month to perform the action outwardly (announcing resignation, signing a contract, moving, applying). You may decide in your heart at any time.
  • The month not to execute means not to perform that action in that month. It does not mean "wait until that month."
  • For actions without a month of strong push, the runner-up month is clearly stated as "There is no strong push, but if executing, do it this month." We do not write advice that leaves you wondering what to do.
  • We do not make definitive statements regarding health, lifespan, medical, legal, or investment outcomes. We also do not guarantee the feelings of others or the success or failure of events.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is the difference from the Lifetime Report (one-off purchase)?
The Lifetime Report (one-off purchase) is a book that reads the unchanging birth chart (Janma Kundali) itself (nature, talents, connections, work, life cycles). The Annual Fortune Calendar reads how "this year's planets" touch that birth chart (Janma Kundali) for 12 months, naming the months to execute decisions. It is the blueprint and this year's clock.
Q. Even if a "month to execute" is indicated, my mind is not yet made up.
The month to execute means "if you move in that month, the stars are pushing you," and it does not force you to move. You are the one who decides; the stars indicate the timing.
Q. What is the period?
It covers 12 months, with the month following your application as the first month (e.g., October → November to next October). It is not a calendar year.
Q. I don't know my time of birth.
Please apply with just your date and place of birth. It can be created because it is read based on the Moon, but the accuracy of some chapters involving house determination will decrease.
Q. What if there are only bad months?
For months not to execute, we write the basis for why they should be avoided. Having months to avoid also means the months to move become clearer.
